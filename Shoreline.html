About this map

Low spots: BCDC (San Francisco Bay Conservation and Development Commission), Adapting to Rising Tides maps, 2017, shoreline overtopping (Bay Shoreline Flood Explorer). Marsh, mudflats and ponds: SFEI Baylands Habitat Map 2020. Buildings and streets: San Mateo County GIS. Critical facilities and transportation: BCDC Regional Shoreline Adaptation Plan (RSAP) data. Flood zones (when switched on): FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer: the 100-year zone has a 1% chance of flooding in any year, the 500-year zone a 0.2% chance. Tides: NOAA tide gauge 9414523, Redwood City. Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors; satellite: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics.
