On the ground: closures, flooding and damage

Everything in date order, each with where it came from. Caltrans: state highways in San Mateo County from the Lane Closure System. City posts: San Bruno's feeds and South San Francisco's emergency page (SMC Alert road closures too, once its mailbox is set up). Staff log: what staff added that no feed reports (lookups/impacts.csv; add a row with its date, place, what happened and the source). A closure's cause is set by a rule, not by Caltrans: high tide when the measured high tide near it ran at least 1 foot above the average high tide, rain when at least 0.25 inch fell in the 12 hours before it closed through 3 hours after, both, or not known.

How this page is made

The facts build themselves through the month from what the Flood Early Warning site already records: the NOAA tide gauges at Redwood City, Alameda, San Francisco and Monterey (heights compared with each gauge's average high tide, MHHW, and ranked against its full record); each storm at each creek gauge (rain, how rare its heaviest part was against NOAA Atlas 14, and the creek levels and statuses reached); and road closures. The month before is updated through the 3rd of the next month, then kept as it is. The words at the top are written by staff and saved in lookups/monthly/. Each city's supervisorial district is from the county's 2022 district map (lookups/places.csv).

Not here yet: community flood reports (none were approved this month), city road closures other than San Bruno's and South San Francisco's, and NWS rain products. Tide numbers are preliminary until NOAA reviews them.
