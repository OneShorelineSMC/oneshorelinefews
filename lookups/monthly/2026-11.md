# November 2026

November brought one large storm, two smaller ones, and the highest tides of the season. On Nov 25 the Bay at Redwood City came within 2 inches of the highest water on record there, and the ocean at Monterey set a new record (preliminary until NOAA reviews it). High water closed state highway ramps in Belmont and Menlo Park, with rain in Belmont.

## King tides, Nov 22 to 25

The Bay rose higher each morning. At Redwood City it peaked at 11:48 AM on Nov 25, 2.5 feet above the average high tide and about a foot above the tide table. Only three days since gauge records began there in 1974 have been higher: Jan 3, 2026, Dec 3, 1983, and Dec 14, 2024. San Francisco had its highest November tide in 109 years of record, and Alameda its highest in 48. On the coast, Monterey beat its January 1983 record by about 2 inches.

Caltrans closed four ramps on SR-84 at the Wildlife Refuge exit in Menlo Park on the morning of Nov 24; they were still closed on Nov 25. The National Weather Service issued Coastal Flood Advisories on 18 of the first 25 days and a Coastal Flood Warning for Nov 25 and 26.

## The storm of Nov 18 to 21

This was the wettest storm of the season so far. La Honda measured 4.9 inches and Pilarcitos Dam 4.5. San Bruno's gauge at First Avenue measured 4.4 inches. A storm that size has about a 1-in-16 (6%) chance in any year.

Atherton Channel reached its Flood Warning level just before noon on Nov 18, the point at which flooding is likely. At Belmont Creek, whose creek gauge was out for repair until that evening, heavy rain alone set the status to "Flooding likely" that morning; once the gauge was back, the creek reached Flood Watch early on Nov 19. San Bruno and Colma Creeks reached Flood Watch. Caltrans closed the southbound US-101 on-ramp at Marine and Harbor in Belmont from the afternoon of Nov 18 to late morning Nov 19, during heavy rain and a high tide.

## Earlier storms

On Nov 13, nearly 2 inches fell at San Bruno's First Avenue gauge while a high tide ran 1.6 feet above average. San Bruno Creek reached Flood Watch and Colma Creek reached Flood Monitoring. The next day SR-1 in Pacifica closed for flooding between Crespi Drive and Linda Mar Boulevard. A smaller storm on Nov 15 and 16 put 1 to 1.3 inches on the coast.

## Looking ahead

The California King Tides Project lists the next king tides for Dec 23 to 25 and Jan 21 to 22. This month the Bay ran as much as a foot above the tide table, so the predicted tides can understate what arrives, and a storm on those dates would add to it.
