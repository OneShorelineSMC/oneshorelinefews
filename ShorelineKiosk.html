About this screen

Bayside Tides: NOAA's Redwood City gauge. Cards show each day's highest tide (NOAA projection for about 2 days, then the tide table); blue = above MHHW, the average daily high (higher than usual), red = the provisional flood level. The line under the The National Weather Service row names the next King Tide (the tide table reaches the 1-year tide) or a tide expected to reach the flood level.

Coastside Tides: the same for the ocean at NOAA's Monterey gauge, the nearest with live readings on the open coast south of San Francisco. No coastside flood level yet; King Tide level provisional. Waves are not included and can push water much farther up a beach.

Creeks: water level (stage) at OneShoreline's creek gauges with their flood thresholds, nearby rainfall, and the Bay tide, all on one timeline.

Data: NOAA Tides & Currents, National Weather Service, and the OneShoreline Early Warning System (OneRain). The tide table does not include storm surge. Data refreshes automatically.
