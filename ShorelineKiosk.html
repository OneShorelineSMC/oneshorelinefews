About this screen

Bay Tides: predicted and measured San Francisco Bay water levels at the NOAA tide gauges in Redwood City and Alameda. Red marks tides above Mean Higher High Water (MHHW), the average height of the highest tide each day.

Creeks: water level (stage) at OneShoreline's creek gauges with their flood thresholds, nearby rainfall, and the Bay tide, all on one timeline. Tap the creek buttons to switch creeks, and "thresholds & notes" for what each level means.

Measured above predicted means storms, wind or runoff are pushing water higher than the tide alone. Upcoming high tides will likely run higher too.

In an emergency, call 911.

Data: NOAA Tides & Currents, National Weather Service, and the OneShoreline Early Warning System (OneRain). Predicted tides do not include storm surge. Data refreshes automatically.
