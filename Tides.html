San Mateo County
Measured and predicted Bay water levels at the NOAA tide gauges in Redwood City and Alameda, and the times high tides are predicted to rise above MHHW in the next 7 days.

MHHW (Mean Higher High Water) is the average height of the highest tide each day. Tides above it reach farther up shorelines, marshes and low-lying areas than usual.

Measured above predicted means storms, wind or runoff are pushing the Bay higher than the tide alone. Upcoming high tides will likely run higher too.

Predicted tides come from NOAA and don't include storm surge. All heights are in feet, NAVD88. For informational purposes only. Disclaimer · In an emergency, call 911.
