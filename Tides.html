    MHHW (Mean Higher High Water) is the average height of the highest tide each day. Tides above it reach farther up shorelines, marshes and low-lying areas than usual.

    Heights come from one series: measured for what has already happened, then NOAA's model forecast for about 2 days, then the predicted (astronomical) tide. Each value is labeled measured, fcst or (no label) predicted.

    NOAA forecast is model guidance from NOAA's San Francisco Bay Operational Forecast System: the water level at this gauge including wind, air pressure and river flow, about 2 days ahead, updated 4 times a day. It is not an official forecast; NWS advisories take precedence.

    Predicted tides come from NOAA and don't include storm surge. All heights are in feet, NAVD88. For informational purposes only. Disclaimer · In an emergency, call 911.
