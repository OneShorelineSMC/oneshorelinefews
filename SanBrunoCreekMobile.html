About this page

San Bruno Creek water level (stage) at First Avenue, measured by OneShoreline's creek gauge, with its flood thresholds: Flood Monitoring 2.40 ft, Flood Watch 3.60 ft and Flood Warning 4.80 ft. These are gauge levels, not National Weather Service alerts.

Rainfall on San Bruno Mountain (upper watershed) and at First Avenue (lower watershed), and the Alameda tide in San Francisco Bay. The creek at First Avenue rises and falls with the tide, so a rise can come from rain, a high tide, or both.

Measured above predicted tide means storms, wind or runoff are pushing the Bay higher than the tide alone. Upcoming high tides will likely run higher too.

In an emergency, call 911.
